# Content and Brand Guide

## Positioning

L&T Consulting and Design is a local web design, AI automation, and on-site SEO business founded by Luke Wilkins and Tim Pactwa.

The voice should be:

- Clear
- Confident
- Honest
- Modern
- Direct
- Not corporate

Avoid sounding like a generic agency template. The strongest angle is not "we are students." The strongest angle is "we are a lean local team that gives direct attention, modern execution, and honest pricing."

The student/founder story can still be useful, but it should usually appear after the visitor already understands the offer.

## Main Messages

Use these ideas consistently:

- Modern websites for local businesses
- Honest pricing
- No agency markup
- Fast, responsive, performance-focused builds
- Practical AI help for real business tasks
- Founder-led, lean, and professional
- Morris County, NJ focus
- On-site SEO included with website builds

## Copy Rules

Write for a busy business owner.

Good copy:

- Says what we do clearly
- Explains business value
- Uses concrete examples
- Keeps paragraphs short
- Avoids buzzwords unless they are explained

Avoid:

- Overpromising AI outcomes
- Vague claims like "revolutionary solutions"
- Long abstract mission statements
- Fake case studies presented as real work
- Enterprise language that does not fit a local agency

## Pricing

Current tiers:

- Starter: `$300-500` plus `$30-50/mo`
- Standard: `$600-900` plus `$50-75/mo`
- Premium: `$1,000-1,500` plus `$75-150/mo`

Recommendation: these prices are low for custom website work, but they can make sense as founding-client pricing while the portfolio is still being built.

A stronger pricing direction:

- Starter site: `$750-1,250`
- Growth site: `$1,500-2,500`
- Premium site: `$3,000-5,000`
- Maintenance: `$75-200/mo`, optional at first
- Simple automation add-on: `$500-1,500`
- More involved automation system: quoted after discovery

Best near-term approach:

- Keep transparent founding-client pricing if needed to close early clients.
- Accept a `$500` minimum for the right founding client if the project creates useful proof, a testimonial, or a strong portfolio example.
- Show that prices will rise as portfolio depth increases.
- Avoid pricing too low because it may signal low quality and leave no room for revisions, onboarding, meetings, and support.
- Keep maintenance affordable at first, but define what is included so it does not become unlimited open-ended work.

Pricing should stay transparent. If prices change, update `lib/data.ts` and any related copy.

## AI Automation Packaging

Recommendation: do not bundle custom AI automation into every website package by default.

Better starting model:

- Every website project can include a brief automation opportunity review.
- Simple automations can be sold as add-ons.
- Standalone automation work can be accepted if the problem is clear and the project is likely to create a useful proof of concept.

This keeps the first goal focused: land the first client and produce real proof. If the first client wants a website, sell the website. If the first client has a clear automation pain point, sell the automation. Do not force the two together before the market shows which offer is easier to close.

Suggested early packaging:

- Website only: priced by site scope.
- Website plus simple automation: site price plus `$250-750` founding-client automation add-on.
- Standalone automation proof of concept: `$500+` if the workflow is narrow and useful.
- Larger automation system: quote after discovery.

Good automation add-ons:

- Contact form routing and automatic follow-up email
- Lead intake form connected to a spreadsheet or CRM
- Review request email sequence
- Simple customer inquiry triage
- Internal checklist or document generation
- Basic reporting dashboard from collected form data

Avoid custom AI promises that depend on unclear data, private systems, or complicated integrations until the business has stronger delivery experience.

## Turnaround Time

Recommended public promise:

```text
Most websites launch in 2-4 weeks after we receive your content and project information.
```

Internal working model:

- Intake and information collection: 2-4 business days
- Website strategy and structure: 1-2 business days
- Website design and development: 5-10 business days
- Review and revisions: 2-5 business days
- AI automation discovery and setup: 5-10 business days depending on scope

Avoid promising "one week" publicly until the process is proven. A one-week build is possible for a simple site, but client feedback, missing photos, copy decisions, and revisions usually stretch timelines.

## Visual Direction

The site should feel polished, modern, and focused.

Current visual language:

- White backgrounds
- Charcoal text
- Blue accent
- Strong typography
- Clean spacing
- Minimal decorative elements
- Subtle motion

Avoid making the site feel too playful, cluttered, or like a SaaS landing page unrelated to local services.
