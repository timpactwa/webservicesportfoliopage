# Project Goal

## What We Are Building

This project is a public-facing website for L&T Consulting and Design, a local web design, AI automation, and on-site SEO business founded by Luke Wilkins and Tim Pactwa.

The first version should act as a credible portfolio and lead-generation site. It does not need to be huge. It needs to be clear, polished, fast, and convincing enough that a local business owner would book a call.

## Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Local small business owners in Morris County, NJ
- Businesses with outdated websites or no real web presence
- Owners who want practical help, not a large agency retainer
- Businesses that would benefit from simple automations but do not know where to start

Secondary audience:

- Early portfolio viewers
- Friends, mentors, professors, and potential referral sources
- Future collaborators or partners

## Core Promise

L&T Consulting and Design helps local businesses modernize their online presence and internal workflows with websites, on-site SEO, and practical AI automations.

The site should make the offer feel:

- Professional
- Direct
- Affordable
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Local

## Positioning Recommendation

Do not lead with "student-run" as the main headline.

Recommended angle:

- Lead with the business outcome: better websites, better local visibility, and less manual work.
- Use the founder story as supporting proof: Luke Wilkins and Tim Pactwa are a lean, founder-led team that can give clients direct attention without agency overhead.
- Mention the student/founder angle on the About page, in sales conversations, and where it supports price transparency.

This avoids making the offer feel inexperienced while still using the real advantage: low overhead, high effort, direct communication, and a modern technical approach.

Suggested phrasing:

```text
L&T Consulting and Design is a lean local team founded by Luke Wilkins and Tim Pactwa. We build modern websites and practical automations for Morris County businesses without the overhead of a traditional agency.
```

## Success Criteria

The project is working when:

- A visitor understands what L&T Consulting and Design does within the first screen.
- Pricing and services are easy to find.
- The contact flow feels real and reliable.
- The site looks better than typical local business websites.
- The codebase is simple enough to keep improving quickly.

## Near-Term Priorities

1. Replace placeholder business details with real information.
2. Connect the contact form to a real submission flow.
3. Add real or sample portfolio work with strong visuals.
4. Improve mobile polish and visual quality across every page.
5. Prepare for deployment and a real domain.
6. Build a repeatable outreach, intake, onboarding, payment, and delivery system.

## Out of Scope for Now

Avoid adding complexity before the core site is strong:

- Client login
- CMS
- Blog engine
- Custom dashboard
- Complex animation systems
- Full AI product features inside the website

Those can come later if the business needs them.

## Current Operating Assumptions

- Starting market: Morris County and nearby areas.
- Expansion market: broader New Jersey and other local-service regions later.
- Lead source: cold outreach to local businesses.
- Initial lead dataset: local town business directories, with manual verification before outreach.
- First lead source: `https://randolphlocal.com/directory/`.
- First outreach segment: businesses with no website listed, then manually verify whether they truly have no website.
- Primary website CTA: `Book a Call`.
- Founding-client minimum: `$500` when the project creates useful proof or a strong first case study.
- Maintenance: optional for now, may become packaged or required later.
- Maintenance scope: hosting support, small edits, basic reports, and on-site tweaks, priced affordably at first.
- Founding-client maintenance price: `$50/mo`.
- Booking stack: Google Calendar plus Google Meet.
- Intake stack: Google Forms first, Typeform later if a more polished intake experience becomes worth the cost.
- Information collection: client intake should happen through a structured form after a call or after a serious inquiry.
- Delivery target: roughly 2-4 weeks depending on scope, client responsiveness, and automation complexity.
- Strategy should be revisited after 2-3 weeks of real outreach and planning.
